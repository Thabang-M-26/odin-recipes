Cape Malay Chicken Curry

Cape Malay chicken curry is a fragrant, mildly spiced South African dish known for balancing savory warmth with subtle sweet notes like apricot jam or chutney.

Ingredients

  • 1 kg chicken pieces (bone-in thighs or drumsticks)
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 2 large onions, finely chopped
  • 4 garlic cloves, minced
  • 2 tbsp fresh ginger, grated
  • Whole spices: 1 cinnamon stick, 3 cardamom pods, 4 cloves, 2 bay leaves
  • Ground spices: 2 tbsp Cape Malay curry powder (or a mix of coriander, cumin, and turmeric), 1 tsp chili powder
  • 400g canned chopped tomatoes or 2 large fresh tomatoes
  • 2 large potatoes, peeled and quartered
  • 1 to 2 tbsp apricot jam or fruity chutney
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • Fresh coriander for garnish

Steps

  1. Sauté Aromatics: Heat oil in a large pot over medium heat and add the whole spices (cinnamon, cardamom, cloves, and bay leaves) until fragrant.
  2. Brown Onions: Add chopped onions and sauté until golden brown, then stir in the garlic and ginger.
  3. Bloom Spices: Stir in the curry powder, turmeric, and cumin, cooking for about one minute so the oils release.
  4. Add Chicken and Tomatoes: Add the chicken pieces and brown them slightly, then pour in the chopped tomatoes, salt, and a splash of water or stock.
  5. Simmer with Potatoes: Add the potatoes and apricot jam or chutney. Cover and simmer on low heat for 30 to 40 minutes until the chicken is tender and the potatoes are soft.
  6. Garnish and Serve: Remove from heat, garnish with fresh coriander, and serve warm.
